# Assembly order

Generated by `build.py` from `BUILD_ORDER` in `model.py`.

Every step below has been checked against the model: the part can travel its
insertion path without passing through anything fitted before it.

1. Screw the two grip halves together with the three M3 x 20 screws.
2. Glue the rear drum flange onto the hex spigot of the drum core.
3. Push the 90 mm axle through the drum.
4. Slip the recoil shield onto the axle behind the drum, tab at the top.
5. Lay the right receiver half on the table, outer face down.
6. Lower the grip so its tongue drops into the back of the receiver.
7. Drop the trigger into its pocket.
8. Lower the drum, axle and shield in together. The axle ends sit in their grooves and the shield's tab sits in the slot under the strap.
9. Lay the rear end of the 345 mm spine rod in its groove at the front of the receiver.
10. Close the left receiver half over it and drive in the eight M3 x 30 screws from the right side. The drum should spin freely.
11. Slide the rear barrel sleeve down the spine rod onto the receiver. Glue it.
12. Slide the cross hub on behind it. Glue it.
13. Push the 203 mm rod through the hub from the right.
14. Slide the right arm over the rod into the hub. Glue it.
15. Slide the left arm on the same way.
16. Glue a ringed cap into the end of the right arm.
17. Glue the other ringed cap into the left arm.
18. Slide the front barrel sleeve down the spine rod into the hub. Glue it.
19. Glue the muzzle cap into the end of the barrel.
20. Glue the pedestal to the top of the rear sleeve, 76 mm ahead of the receiver.
21. Set the torch in the pedestal with the bell facing forward. Glue it.
22. Glue the cross sight into its slot at the muzzle.
23. Offer the twisted rod up under the barrel and push it back into the socket at the front of the receiver.
24. Glue the small saddle onto the Dragon's Breath tube, its rear edge 16 mm from the socket end.
25. Offer the tube up under the hub, saddle against the hub, then slide it back 10 mm onto the twisted rod. Glue the saddle to the hub.
26. Load a shell through the gate on the right side of the recoil shield, turn the drum one chamber and repeat for all twelve.
